### 1. What We Do (Icyo Dukora)
AgrIntel unifies official national harvest surveys, daily town market prices, and rainfall updates across all 30 districts of Rwanda so that **no farmer, trader, or family has to make decisions in the dark**.

### 2. What Value We Bring (Akamaro Tubazaniye)
* **Protects Farmers from Selling at a Loss:** Tells farmers whether local crop prices are rising (**Sell Now**), falling (**Store in Warehouse**), or stable (**Normal Marketing**).
* **Helps Truck Drivers & Traders Earn Profits:** Calculates real diesel fuel costs (32 L / 100 km) and bumpy road food spoilage to find the most profitable towns for surplus harvest.
* **Shields Families from High Food Prices:** The *Smart Food Basket* shows affordable, healthy food swaps that deliver the exact same calories and protein while saving money every month.
* **Assists Sector Leaders & Extension Agronomists:** Compares actual harvests with expected targets given rainfall and fertilizer, spotting counterfeit seeds or distribution delays early.

### 4. 100% Official Data & Grounded Field Verification
* **NISR Seasonal Agricultural Survey (SAS):** Multi-season survey microdata across all 30 districts.
* **Rwanda Meteo & CHIRPS Satellite:** Live district rainfall, consecutive dry spell days, and dairy pasture status.
* **WFP & TangaBid Farmgate:** Verified wholesale, retail, and rural farmgate prices across Rwandan markets.
* **National Statistics Standards:** Aligned with Rwanda’s **NST2 (2024–2029)**, **NSDS4**, and **Vision 2050**.
